01 / The problem

Liquidity risk is a
whole-system event.

A family office does not experience funds, markets, companies, property, debt and facilities in isolation. In a squeeze, they move together. The risk lives in the joint event.

02 / What it does

One system.
One number.

  1. 01

    Models the whole balance sheet as one correlated system.

    Funds, public markets, operating companies, property, debt and facilities move together, because in a squeeze they do.

  2. 02

    Returns one number.

    The probability of a forced sale or a facility draw over the next 24 months.

  3. 03

    Tests the decision before it is made.

    Drop a purchase or a commitment on the book and watch the number move.
